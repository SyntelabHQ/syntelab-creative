# Romanian words that are written with diacritics, as they are written.
# Written by hand for Scribe; lines starting with # are notes.
#
#   word     a form with diacritics
#   =word    a word that is right without diacritics too (so the one above is not the only reading)
#   word~    a feminine noun ending in ă: its form ending in a (the one with the article) is right too, and likelier
#   word^    an adjective or a verb form ending in ă whose form ending in a is right too, but less likely
#
# Where a word can be written in more than one way, the likelier way is written first.
#
